Shasta County houseboat sting arrests eight in a commercial-sex operation

Shasta Lake, California8 arrested

Shasta County sheriff's investigators, working with Redding police and the FBI, used a houseboat on the lake's shoreline as an undercover meeting point over the weekend of Aug. 28-30, arresting eight people. Six were accused of agreeing to pay for sex acts and one man was accused of profiting from a woman's prostitution; an eighth person was booked on an unrelated warrant and a drug charge. Investigators said they offered help to anyone contacted who showed signs of exploitation, but the operation did not report identifying or recovering a trafficking victim.
