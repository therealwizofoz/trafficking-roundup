Operation Meridian recovers 79 missing and endangered children across Ohio

79 recovered · mixed missing-child casesOhio

The U.S. Marshals Service and more than 30 federal, state and local partners spent August running Operation Meridian, locating 79 children ages 6 to 17 who had been reported missing or endangered across Ohio, in what officials called the largest such operation in the state's history. The cases spanned suspected sex trafficking, abuse, neglect and runaways; in Toledo, a 16-year-old was found with an adult man now under investigation for possible trafficking, while in Lockbourne, a 13-year-old missing since 2024 was found hidden in a basement crawlspace.

SourceU.S. Marshals Service

Bangladesh's RAB intercepts a group staged for a trafficking voyage to Malaysia

5 recoveredTeknaf, Cox's Bazar, BangladeshNone; suspects fled

Bangladesh's Rapid Action Battalion rescued five people before dawn on September 7 from a coastal village in Teknaf after a tip that they had been gathered for a boat crossing to Malaysia. The suspected traffickers ran before they could be caught, and the five were handed to local police to be reunited with their families.

SourceThe Business Standard

2National / Federal (US)

Final defendant in a South Carolina teen sex-trafficking ring sentenced to 25 years

Columbia, South CarolinaLast of 4 defendants sentenced

A federal judge in Columbia sentenced Antonio Marquis Nicholson to 25 years for leading a ring that trafficked three teenage girls, among them a runaway and two high school students, for commercial sex across South Carolina, Georgia and North Carolina in 2022 and 2023. He is the last of four defendants sentenced in the case; the victims were recovered in an earlier rescue operation, not this week.

SourceU.S. Attorney's Office, District of South Carolina

3Local & State Cases

Decatur County, Indiana buyer sting charges four out-of-state men with soliciting a minor

Decatur County, Indiana4 charged

The Greensburg Police Department and Decatur County Sheriff's Office arrested four men from Ohio and Kentucky in an undercover sting targeting adults seeking to buy sex from minors, after officers trained specifically on identifying trafficking cases. All four face felony child-solicitation and human-trafficking-promotion charges.

SourceDecatur County Prosecutor's Office

4International

Spanish and Colombian police close a years-long case exploiting Colombians as hospitality workers

Tarragona and Madrid, Spain; Bogotá, Colombia30 arrested across the case

Spain's National Police and Civil Guard, working with Colombian police, announced the close of a nearly two-year case against a network that recruited vulnerable Colombians with false promises of academic and internship programs, then used manufactured debt to trap them in bar and hotel jobs in Tarragona and Madrid. Arrest phases in 2024, 2025 and this month brought the total to 30 people arrested and 57 victims identified; the final arrests, of organizers in Tarragona and Bogotá, were announced this week.

SourceSpain's Ministry of the Interior
