Oklahoma trafficking unit's training exercise turns into a real rescue near Guthrie

3 recoveredGuthrie, Oklahoma5 arrested

A training session the Oklahoma Attorney General's Human Trafficking Response Unit ran with Guthrie police, the Logan County Sheriff's Office and two neighboring departments led directly into a real-world operation on September 11, in which investigators located three potential trafficking victims. Five men were taken into custody; formal charges had not been filed as of the following morning.

SourceGuthrie News Page

Assam-Arunachal police break up a ring moving children into domestic servitude, freeing three girls

3 recovered · 3 minorsItanagar, Arunachal Pradesh, India6 arrested

Police recovered three minor girls, the youngest age seven, from an inter-state trafficking network that brought children from Assam into Arunachal Pradesh to work as unpaid domestic help. One child was found injured in Itanagar's F-Sector; the other two were located separately. Six people have been arrested as investigators work to map the rest of the network.

SourceThe Assam Tribune

Ogun State police free three children from an alleged child-trafficking gang

3 recovered · 3 minorsIbafo, Ogun State, Nigeria3 arrested

Detectives in Ibafo arrested a woman on suspicion of child theft, and following her arrest located two accomplices and freed three children, ages 12 to 16, who police say had been taken and were being held for sale. The case has been transferred to the state Criminal Investigation Department in Abeokuta for further investigation.

SourceGazette NGR

California task force recovers a trafficked 16-year-old from a Bay Area motel

1 recovered · 1 minorOakland, California2 arrested

Stanislaus County's Human Exploitation and Trafficking Task Force arrested two 18-year-old men on September 8 after an investigation that began five days earlier, recovering a 16-year-old girl from a motel room in Oakland. Investigators say the pair also trafficked adult victims for commercial sex during trips to Southern California, Sacramento and other Bay Area cities; both men remain held on $900,000 bail each.

SourceCedar News

Three men plead guilty to running a Baton Rouge-area forced prostitution ring

Baton Rouge, Louisiana3 pleaded guilty

A Mexican national pleaded guilty to sex trafficking by force, fraud or coercion, and a Guatemalan and a second Mexican national pleaded guilty to conspiracy in the same scheme, which prosecutors say advertised women online for commercial sex and rented apartments in Baton Rouge, Kenner and New Orleans since at least August 2024. Many of the women exploited were themselves in the country illegally; sentencing has not yet been scheduled.

SourceU.S. Department of Justice

Treasury and DOJ target the financial network behind a Southeast Asian scam-compound operator

United States

The Treasury Department sanctioned Xinbi Guarantee, a Chinese-language online marketplace it says launders proceeds and moves money for scam compounds that traffic workers into forced criminality across Southeast Asia, along with a Cambodia-based crypto-wallet developer and a Singapore-based messaging-app maker that support its operations. The Justice Department's Scam Center Strike Force simultaneously seized infrastructure and digital-asset wallets tied to the network.

SourceU.S. Department of the Treasury

Tennessee's Columbia massage-parlor raid was one piece of a 10-arrest statewide sweep

Tennessee10 arrested statewide

The Columbia raid reported September 10, which produced two arrests and a $104,000 seizure, turns out to have been one of seven locations hit the same day in a Tennessee Bureau of Investigation operation that also covered Murfreesboro, Chattanooga, Kingsport and Cookeville. Across all five cities, 10 people were arrested on prostitution-related charges, with the state's Human Trafficking Task Force, the FBI and Homeland Security Investigations also identifying workers held for immigration violations while they continue looking for trafficking victims.

SourceWATE
