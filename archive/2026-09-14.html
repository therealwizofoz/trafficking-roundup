Houston task force recovers 12 trafficking victims, most of them minors, in a Bissonnet Track sweep

12 recovered · 9 minorsHouston, Texas10 arrested

A multiagency operation led by Homeland Security Investigations, the FBI and Houston police that ran from August 28 to September 7 targeted traffickers working the Bissonnet Track, a stretch of Houston long associated with commercial sex trafficking. Investigators identified nine minor victims and three adult victims, who were connected with support and recovery services, while arresting ten suspects, including four documented gang members, on federal sex-trafficking charges.

SourceICE/HSI

Washington man admits trafficking two 15-year-old girls between Seattle and Portland

2 minorsPortland, Oregon1 pleaded guilty

Dajohntae Rayshawnlee Richard, 26, pleaded guilty in federal court to two counts of sex trafficking by force, fraud and coercion involving two 15-year-old girls. Prosecutors said he contacted one victim on Facebook in May 2025 and drove her from Washington to Oregon to solicit buyers, then approached a second 15-year-old in the Seattle area weeks later and used threats to keep her compliant; he faces a 15-year mandatory minimum at sentencing on December 7, 2026.

SourceUS Attorney's Office, District of Oregon

4International

Thai police raid a Ratchaburi karaoke bar over the alleged prostitution of underage girls

Ratchaburi, Thailand3 arrested

Thailand's Human Trafficking Suppression Division raided a karaoke venue in Photharam district, Ratchaburi province, on September 14, arresting three women accused of dressing underage girls as paid "drinking companions" and then selling them for sex. Investigators have confirmed at least one victim under 18 and are examining whether other girls, some reportedly as young as 15, were also exploited at the venue.

SourceKhaosod English

Mexican court sentences operator of trafficking website ZonaDivas to 30 years

Mexico City, Mexico

A federal judge in Mexico sentenced Juan Carlos Benavides Cuadros to 30 years in prison for trafficking and aggravated sexual exploitation tied to ZonaDivas, a website that for nearly two decades posted sex advertisements featuring more than 500 Latin American women. Prosecutors showed Benavides personally transported four foreign victims across Mexican states in 2017 to force them into prostitution, and two co-defendants in the same investigation have received comparable sentences.

SourceInfobae México

Lima police arrest a man accused of using fake TikTok modeling scouts to exploit two teenage girls

2 minors identifiedLima, Peru1 arrested

Police arrested 20-year-old Aldair Álvaro Huamaní Inga at a hotel in Lima after he allegedly used fake TikTok profiles posing as talent scouts to lure two girls, ages 15 and 17, with modeling offers, then coerced explicit photos and sex from them while advertising minors through a WhatsApp group. A judge ordered nine months of preventive detention on aggravated-trafficking charges.

SourceInfobae Perú

Amnesty International says Russia's recruitment of foreign fighters for Ukraine amounts to trafficking

Moscow, Russia

Amnesty International said Russia's recruitment of foreign nationals to fight in Ukraine meets the legal definition of human trafficking, citing interviews with prisoners of war and recruits' families describing fake civilian job ads, confiscated passports and phones, and coercion into frontline combat. The group estimates 28,000 foreign nationals from 136 countries, including Brazil, Colombia, Sri Lanka and South Africa, are fighting in the Russian army under such arrangements; the reporting notes Russia has not responded to the findings.

SourceAmnesty International, via Al Jazeera

Dozens of trafficked scam-compound workers remain confined in a Manila-area facility awaiting repatriation

~70 awaiting repatriationPasay, Philippines

The Philippine Anti-Organized Crime Commission is still holding roughly 70 foreign nationals, down from a peak of more than 900, at a converted former scam compound in Pasay, where they remain as material witnesses awaiting prosecutions and repatriation. Detainees interviewed by the Associated Press, mainly from Vietnam, China and Malaysia, described being lured with fake administrative jobs, beaten for underperformance, and in at least one case resold between scam operators for as much as $34,000.

SourceAssociated Press, via BusinessMirror
