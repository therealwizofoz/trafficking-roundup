Nigerian agency recovers more than 180 children sold through fake back-to-school charities

180+ recovered · all minorsBenue, Nasarawa, Kaduna and Anambra states, Nigeria1 arrested, manhunt underway

Nigeria's National Agency for the Prohibition of Trafficking in Persons said it has recovered more than 180 children trafficked out of five states by criminal networks posing as civil-society groups and offering free schooling to families displaced by violence and insecurity. Investigators arrested a nurse in Onitsha, Anambra State, who was found caring for one of the children, and have opened a manhunt for other suspects behind the wider network.

SourceNAPTIP

Kenyan police pull four young children from a suspected trafficker's rented room

4 recovered · ages 1-17Isinya, Kajiado County, Kenya1 arrested

Kenya Police, the Administration Police Service and the Directorate of Criminal Investigations arrested a man on Sept. 4 in Isinya, Kajiado County, and recovered four children between the ages of 1 and 17 who were living in his single rented room. Investigators say his activities may have extended to Mashuru and Nakuru County as well, and the children are undergoing medical evaluation while police work to establish the full scope of the case.

SourceKenya Police Service

2National / Federal (US)

Federal indictment accuses LA gang members of trafficking 51 people, many runaways, on the Figueroa Corridor

Los Angeles, California11 charged

A federal indictment unsealed this week charges 11 reputed members and associates of Los Angeles's Hoover Criminal Gang with racketeering conspiracy for running a sex-trafficking operation on South LA's Figueroa Corridor from February 2021 to August 2025. Prosecutors say the network recruited at least 51 victims, including runaways and children from foster care as young as 14, often through social media, and marked some of them with tattoos.

SourceU.S. Attorney's Office, Central District of California

Second guilty plea in Washington state scheme that recruited 518 Mexican farmworkers under false terms

Kennewick, Washington1 pleaded guilty

Erica Cisneros, a recruiter for the Kennewick-based labor contractor Harvest Plus, pleaded guilty Aug. 27 to fraud in foreign labor contracting and aggravated identity theft. She is the second defendant to plead guilty in a case accusing Harvest Plus of fraudulently recruiting 518 Mexican farmworkers between 2022 and 2024 and placing them under the H-2A visa program in substandard jobs, housing and pay. The company's owner remains jailed awaiting trial, and his wife faces a separate forced-labor charge.

SourceCapital Press

Appeals court upholds $1.4 million verdict against Michigan farm labor contractor for forced labor

Michigan

The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit ruled Aug. 28 to uphold a jury verdict of roughly $1.4 million against Purpose Point Harvesting, finding the Michigan farm labor contractor liable for trafficking and forced-labor violations against five Guatemalan workers brought in on H-2A visas. The workers had testified that the company confiscated their passports, charged illegal recruitment fees and forced them to work up to 19 hours a day between 2017 and 2019 while paying them for only a fraction of those hours.

SourceSixth Circuit Court of Appeals

Alabama sheriff's deputy charged with trafficking and raping his girlfriend under threat of deportation

Blount County, Alabama1 arrested

Blount County authorities arrested Jefferson County sheriff's deputy Robert Nicholas Lisle, 47, on Sept. 1 on charges of second-degree human trafficking, first-degree rape and unlawful imprisonment. Investigators say Lisle threatened to report his girlfriend to immigration authorities to coerce her into sexual servitude between July and August, including a forcible rape on July 15. He has been placed on leave from the Jefferson County Sheriff's Office pending an internal investigation.

SourceTrussville Tribune

New York woman charged with running a Pennsylvania massage parlor as a trafficking operation

North Londonderry Township, Pennsylvania1 charged

The Lebanon County Human Trafficking Task Force charged Na Wei, 41, of Rochester, New York, on Sept. 3 with human trafficking, involuntary servitude and corrupt organizations following an investigation of the New Venus Spa in North Londonderry Township. Investigators say Wei required workers to perform sex acts for money on a seven-day schedule and threatened to fire anyone who refused. The spa is the fifth illicit massage business the task force has shut down in the past ten months.

4International

Brazilian police investigate ring accused of selling an infant across the border into Paraguay

Foz do Iguaçu, Brazil

Brazil's Federal Police opened "Operação Repatriar" on Sept. 4, serving three search warrants in Foz do Iguaçu after a five-month-old baby was allegedly sold and taken into Paraguay in late August as part of what investigators believe is an illegal-adoption trafficking scheme, with part of the payment reportedly made through Brazil's Pix instant-payment system. The infant has since been located and returned, and police are investigating whether a wider network is involved in placing Brazilian children abroad.

SourceSOT
