Nigerian agency recovers the last of 16 children sold by a fake charity's 'back to school' scheme

16 minors recoveredNasarawa State, NigeriaBuyers and ringleader held

Nigeria's Civil Defence Corps says it has recovered the last of 16 children who were taken from families in Nasarawa State in January by a syndicate posing as an NGO's back-to-school assistance program and sold to buyers around the country for ₦2 million each. Fifteen of the children were traced to buyers across eastern Nigeria in recent weeks; the final child was recovered in Kwali, on the outskirts of Abuja, where three buyers were taken into custody. The alleged ringleader and other suspects were arrested earlier in the case.

SourceGazette NGR

Alabama man pleads guilty mid-trial after the minor he trafficked to Illinois testifies against him

Rockford, Illinois1 pleaded guilty

James Price, 39, of Bay Minette, Alabama, pleaded guilty in federal court in Rockford, Illinois, midway through his trial on charges of conspiring to commit sex trafficking, causing a minor to engage in a commercial sex act, and producing and distributing child sexual abuse material. He entered the plea after several government witnesses, including the minor victim, had already testified about being brought to Illinois to meet him. Price faces a mandatory minimum of 15 years and up to life in prison at sentencing, set for January 25, 2027.

SourceU.S. Attorney's Office, N.D. Illinois

Houston-area man sentenced to over 11 years for paying a 16-year-old for sex acts and images

Spring, Texas1 sentenced

A federal judge in Houston sentenced Spring, Texas resident John Alberth, 53, to 135 months in prison for the sex trafficking of a minor. Prosecutors said that from August through October 2023, Alberth exchanged messages with a 16-year-old girl, paid her through a mobile payment app for sexual activity, and asked her to send images that constituted child sexual abuse material. He will serve five years of supervised release with restrictions on contact with children and internet use, and must register as a sex offender.

SourceU.S. Attorney's Office, S.D. Texas

3Local & State Cases

Four-day Florida hotel sting nets 50 arrests and identifies trafficking victims near Vero Beach

Several victims identifiedIndian River County, Florida50 arrested

The Florida Department of Law Enforcement and the Indian River County Sheriff's Office ran a four-day undercover operation, "Operation Late Checkout," at a hotel outside Vero Beach from September 10 through 13, arresting 50 people on prostitution- and drug-related charges. The sheriff's office said the operation identified several human trafficking victims during the sweep, though it did not give a count, and that those cases remain under investigation.

SourceSebastian Daily

Denton County trafficking unit raids two Lewisville massage parlors, identifies three victims

3 identifiedLewisville, Texas1 arrested

The Denton County Sheriff's Office Human Trafficking Unit and Lewisville police arrested Shujun Sun on a trafficking-of-persons warrant on September 10, following a months-long investigation into two massage businesses, Zen Health Therapy Spa and Massage 7. Three Chinese national women found at the businesses were identified as potential trafficking victims and referred to a local victim-services agency; investigators seized more than $8,000 in cash and business records.

4International

UK sentences five in a Vietnamese smuggling ring that held migrants in debt bondage

United Kingdom5 sentenced

A Eurojust-coordinated investigation led to the conviction and sentencing of five people in the United Kingdom for running a network that smuggled Vietnamese migrants into the country and laundered more than €1.8 million in proceeds. Migrants were charged as much as €20,000 for the journey and then required to work for the criminal group to pay off the debt, a coercive arrangement that moves the case beyond smuggling into forced labor. Sentences ranged from four to twelve years for smuggling and two to eight years for money laundering; related raids in March 2026 led to 16 arrests in France and three in Belgium.

SourceEurojust

UK formally submits its extradition request for the Tate brothers

Miami, Florida

British prosecutors met a September 16 court-imposed deadline to submit a complete extradition request to the U.S. State Department for Andrew and Tristan Tate, who have been fighting the case from federal custody in Miami since a judge denied their release on September 9. The State Department is now reviewing the filing for compliance with the U.S.-U.K. extradition treaty, a required step before the case can reach a U.S. court; the brothers separately face a Romanian indictment on charges of trafficking minors and money laundering.

SourceAssociated Press
