Ghana immigration raid removes 1,055 people, most of them children, from a trafficking-linked begging network

1,055 recovered · 662 minorsAccra and Kumasi, Ghana

Ghana's Immigration Service raided organized street-begging operations across Accra and Kumasi in the early hours of 29 August, removing 1,055 West African nationals — 662 of them children — from networks accused of trafficking and controlling vulnerable migrants for forced begging. Several Ghanaian nationals suspected of acting as local agents for the networks were also held for questioning, and a pregnant woman among those recovered went into labor during the operation and was taken to a hospital, where she delivered safely. The service said the group has been moved to a holding center for profiling and is working with the Ministry of Gender and Social Protection to arrange care for the children.

SourceThe Ghana Report

US Marshals find a second, unreported missing teenager while rescuing a trafficked 16-year-old in Arizona

2 recovered · 2 minorsTolleson, Arizona

The U.S. Marshals Service, searching a Tolleson home on 26 August for a 16-year-old who had been missing since mid-June, found her along with a second, previously unreported missing teenager; investigators say both were being exploited for commercial sex. The Marshals worked with Phoenix police and Arizona's Department of Child Safety to connect the girls with recovery services. No arrests had been announced as of the 30 August update, and Phoenix police's trafficking unit continues investigating who was responsible.

SourceFOX 10 Phoenix

Argentine officials free four farmworkers held in unsanitary conditions near La Rioja

4 recoveredLa Rioja, Argentina1 held

Argentine officials raided a property on Provincial Route 5 outside the city of La Rioja and removed four adults — three men and one woman, all from Santiago del Estero province — who told investigators they had been made to work for about ten days without adequate sanitation or hygiene facilities. One person connected to the property was held pending the human-trafficking investigation.

SourceGovernment of Argentina

Fulton County task force locates a trafficked teenager reported missing from North Carolina

1 recovered · 1 minorEast Point, Georgia1 arrested

After Fayetteville, North Carolina police reported a teenage girl missing and asked for help, Fulton County Sheriff's Office investigators traced online commercial-sex advertisements featuring her to metro Atlanta and recovered her in East Point on 26 August, working with a SWAT team and Homeland Security Investigations. One suspect was arrested; the sheriff's office has not released the person's name or the charges while the investigation continues.

SourceWSB-TV

2National / Federal (US)

Aurora forced-labor ring indicted over migrants held to work off smuggling debts

Aurora, Colorado7 charged

A federal grand jury in Denver indicted seven people on 25 August, charging them with smuggling six victims into the United States between October 2021 and July 2023 and then forcing them to work off their debts under threats and abuse in Aurora. Six of the defendants face conspiracy, forced-labor and alien-smuggling counts; a seventh faces one count each of forced labor and alien smuggling. The FBI's Denver field office, IRS Criminal Investigation and the Colorado State Patrol investigated the case.

SourceU.S. Attorney's Office, District of Colorado

3Local & State Cases

Anaheim buyer sting nets ten arrests and identifies a suspected pimp

Anaheim, California10 arrested

The Orange County Human Trafficking Task Force and Anaheim police arrested 10 people on 27 August in a demand-side operation targeting buyers of commercial sex, and identified one suspected pimp for further investigation. The California Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control and multiple Anaheim police units took part; no victim count was reported.

SourceNew Santa Ana
