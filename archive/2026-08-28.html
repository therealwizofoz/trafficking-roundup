1Victims Recovered

Florida sweep locates 163 missing children in five days

163 recovered · all minorsFlorida

A five-day statewide operation announced on 25 August located 163 missing children across Florida, with the largest numbers in the Tampa Bay region (46), Miami (41) and Jacksonville (32). State officials said the children ranged from two months to 17 years old and that many had been exploited, abused or neglected; the state has not published a separate count of how many were confirmed trafficking victims. Children were traced to locations in other states and, officials said, a dozen other countries.

SourceWFSU News

FBI's two-week national sweep finds 84 exploited children and 141 adult victims

225 recovered · 84 minorsNationwide85 arrested or identified

The FBI's annual Operation Cross Country, run in the first two weeks of August with local police departments, located 84 child victims of exploitation and 141 adult trafficking victims, and found 37 children who were actively missing at the time. Eighty-five people were arrested or identified on child exploitation and trafficking offences. In Phoenix, where the local phase ran 9–12 August, agents and the city police department's human exploitation unit located 17 adult victims and made no arrests.

SourceYahoo News

Marshals-led operation recovers 48 endangered children in Massachusetts

48 recovered · all minorsMassachusetts

Operation Yellow Card, announced on 10 August, recovered 48 missing children aged 12 to 17 who investigators considered at heightened risk of trafficking and commercial sexual exploitation. Some were found outside Massachusetts. The Marshals worked with state and Boston police, Homeland Security Investigations, the FBI, three district attorneys' offices and the state child welfare and youth services agencies, which took over care of the children afterward.

SourceU.S. Marshals Service

Nineteen children, most under eight, taken back from a placement network in northeast India

19 recovered · all minorsArunachal Pradesh, India3 arrested

Police in Arunachal Pradesh recovered 19 children in a coordinated sweep across six towns reported on 25 August, and arrested three women accused of arranging the placements. The youngest child was about three and most were under eight. Investigators said families were approached with promises of schooling and care, and the children were then placed in distant districts to work in homes and hotels. A special investigation team was formed because the case crosses district lines.

SourceThe Assam Tribune

2National / Federal (US)

Homeland Security reports 180 victims recovered and more than 900 arrests across the World Cup

180 recovered · 30 minorsUS host cities900+ arrested

In a 29 July accounting of enforcement around the 38-day tournament, Homeland Security said federal agencies arrested more than 900 suspected traffickers and recovered 180 victims, 30 of them children. The San Francisco Bay Area operation accounted for 53 identified victims, 11 of them minors, and 25 arrests; Kansas City reported nine children located and nine people referred to victim services. ICE, the FBI and CBP worked the host cities alongside local departments.

SourceU.S. Department of Homeland Security

Flooring manufacturer's owner charged with forced labor of Chinese workers in Georgia

Cartersville, Georgia3 charged

Federal prosecutors in the Northern District of Georgia charged the 60-year-old owner of Wellmade Industries and two associates on 12 August with conspiracy to commit forced labor, forced labor and, for one defendant, alien harboring. The indictment alleges Chinese nationals brought in on business and intra-company visas were held to 12-hour shifts, six days a week, in unsafe conditions, and kept in place with threats of violence, deportation and debt. The FBI's Atlanta field office had raided the plant in March 2025. Each forced labor count carries up to 20 years.

SourceFOX 5 Atlanta

Five men charged in Omaha after an undercover child sex trafficking operation

Omaha, Nebraska5 charged

Homeland Security Investigations announced charges on 19 August against five Nebraska men who, prosecutors say, answered online advertisements believing they were arranging sex with a child. Three are registered sex offenders, which raises the penalties they face, and one is also charged with possessing child sexual abuse material. No child was involved: investigators placed the advertisements and handled the exchanges themselves. Omaha police, the Colorado Bureau of Investigation and the Nebraska Attorney General's office took part.

SourceU.S. Department of Justice

3Local & State Cases

Two-month Hillsborough County operation ends with 116 arrests and five victims out

5 recovered · 2 minorsHillsborough County, Florida116 arrested

Operation Relentless Summer, which ran from 8 June to 5 August and was announced on 12 August, produced 116 arrests in Hillsborough County and recovered five trafficking victims — two 16-year-old girls and three women. The sheriff's office ran it with the Florida Attorney General's office, and two victim services organisations took over aftercare.

SourceFOX 13 Tampa Bay

6 recoveredComal County, Texas6 arrested

The Comal County Sheriff's Office said on 6 August that a trafficking sting north of San Antonio recovered six people and led to six arrests. The office released little else about how the operation was run or what charges the six face.

4International

Five-day operation across 59 countries identifies 2,070 trafficking victims

59 countries1,024 arrested

Operation Global Chain, run 8–12 June and reported on 6 July, identified 2,070 victims across 59 countries and produced 1,024 arrests, 334 of them for trafficking offences. Austria and Romania led it, with command posts in Skopje and Rio de Janeiro. Victims came mainly from Argentina, Colombia, Venezuela, Moldova and Nepal; about one in ten was a minor. Brazil's federal police broke up a network that had moved 406 people to Cambodia to work in online scam operations. These are victims identified in screening rather than a count of people removed from captivity, so they are not added to this site's recovery tally.

SourceEuropol

Four Nigerian teenagers taken back from a route to Côte d'Ivoire

4 recovered · minorsCross River State, Nigeria

Police in Cross River State said on 11 August that they had recovered four teenage girls from a trafficking route running to Côte d'Ivoire. Two were repatriated after being taken abroad; two more were intercepted in Lagos before they left the country. Officers said the families had been approached with offers of work and opportunity abroad. No arrest figure was released.

SourceThe Guardian Nigeria

5Ongoing Cases with New Developments

Michigan forced labor prosecution of a self-styled apostle slips to 2027

Michigan, Florida, Texas, Missouri

A year after the arrest of a 53-year-old church leader and his business partner on forced labor and money laundering charges, the case has been pushed to February 2027 or later under the weight of 45 terabytes of discovery, according to a 27 August review of the docket. Prosecutors say people were made to work unpaid in donation call centres across four states and as personal attendants, with their movement and communication controlled. A third defendant was charged in February. The defence twice asked for release this month, on 7 and 14 August, arguing newly disclosed evidence weakens the case; prosecutors are opposing.

SourceWDIV Local 4
