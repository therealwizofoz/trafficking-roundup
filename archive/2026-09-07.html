Bihar teenager traced and pulled out of a forced marriage in Rajasthan

1 recovered · minorChuru district, Rajasthan, India1 arrested, second suspect sought

Police in Bihar's Kaimur district traced and recovered a teenage girl from Rajasthan after her family reported she had been taken from their village in December on the promise of a job and then sold into marriage for roughly 200,000 rupees. A special team traveled to Rajasthan's Churu district, rescued the girl and arrested the man accused of buying her; investigators are still searching for the broker accused of arranging the sale.

SourceKaimur district police, Bihar

Eight men from a Missouri county's online-predator sting face federal trafficking charges

Boone County, Missouri8 charged

Federal prosecutors in Missouri's Western District elevated the cases of eight men to federal court this week, charging each with attempted enticement of a minor and attempted sex trafficking of a minor. The men were first arrested in a Boone County undercover sting targeting adults seeking to meet children online for sex.

SourceU.S. Attorney's Office, Western District of Missouri

3Local & State Cases

Ohio sheriff charges two men with running a labor-trafficking network near Oberlin

Lorain County, Ohio2 charged, roughly 50 held on immigration violations

After a year-long investigation, the Lorain County Sheriff's Office and federal immigration agents stopped vehicles along a rural stretch of U.S. 20 near Oberlin and charged two men who allegedly ran a smuggling and labor-placement operation supplying workers to farms across the county under the cover of transportation and contracting businesses. Roughly 50 other people were taken into federal custody on civil immigration violations while investigators work to determine how many were trafficking victims.

SourceLorain County Sheriff's Office

4International

Romanian prosecutors add minor-trafficking charges against Andrew Tate

Bucharest, Romania

Romanian organized-crime prosecutors indicted Andrew Tate on charges of trafficking a minor and money laundering, and indicted his brother Tristan Tate on a related complicity charge, alleging the pair coerced women recruited under false promises of relationships into producing pornographic content and kept most of the proceeds. One case detailed in the indictment involves a girl prosecutors say was 15 when Andrew Tate began the relationship in Britain in 2012. The brothers, already jailed in Florida awaiting extradition to the UK on separate rape charges, deny the allegations.

SourceDIICOT (Romanian organized crime and terrorism prosecutors)

5Ongoing Cases with New Developments

Arunachal Pradesh child-trafficking probe grows to 38 traced, a fourth arrest made

Namsai district, Arunachal Pradesh, India4th suspect arrested

The Arunachal Pradesh trafficking investigation logged here on August 28, when 19 children were traced and three women held, has grown: police now say they have traced 38 people in total, 34 of them children, across six towns in Arunachal Pradesh and neighboring Assam, and have made a fourth arrest, of a woman accused of transporting and placing victims. The case remains open as police trace financial records and additional suspects.

SourceNamsai Police
